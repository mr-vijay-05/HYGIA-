Hygia Patient Assistance & FAQs

Frequently asked questions and emergency guidance

How does Hygia protect my personal health records?

Hygia uses 256-bit encryption for all uploaded PDFs, images and extracted biomarkers. Your records are never sold or shared with advertisers.

What is the difference between Patient and Family Member login?

Patient accounts are the primary owners of health records. Family accounts allow authorized caregivers to review records, track visits and view timelines with patient permission.

How do I create a new account?

Click Create a new account at the bottom of the sign-in card. Enter your full name, email or phone, and choose whether you are a primary patient or a family caregiver to immediately set up your portal.

Medical Disclaimer: Hygia is an AI health organizer, not an emergency medical provider. If you are experiencing an acute medical emergency, please call 911 or visit your nearest emergency room immediately.
